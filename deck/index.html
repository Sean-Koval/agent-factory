How the team gets better

Change the harness on evidence, not impressions

  1. Change a skill, hook or plugin in the harness
  2. Freeze a good run into harness.lock
  3. Experiment: stable against candidate, paired
  4. Report the verdict with intervals on the PR
  5. Promote: move stable; drift checks guard it
Infrastructure failures are retried and left out of every statistic8 of 14
What the team sees · flightdeck

The verdict lands on the harness PR

flightdeck posts one comment on the pull request that changes the harness and updates it in place: the verdict, both variants' pins, the outcome and its interval.

  • Candidate is better than control on resolved: +21.2 pp, CI +1.5 to +43.9.
  • Paired by task; infrastructure failures are excluded and listed.

Governance

Risky actions fail closed and leave a receipt

TaintTracked

Reading a web page, a remote MCP server or a ticket marks the run.

PushFetch-only

Sandboxes can't push. hangar delivers one commit through its gateway.

Decision pointAsk first

Release, landing, egress and secrets go to Tower: allow, deny or wait.

ApproverNever you

The requester never approves their own risky work. Every decision is signed.

Rollout: off, then shadow, then enforce, one decision kind at a time10 of 14
What the team sees · Tower

The Desk: what needs you, what finished, who you approve for

Tower's home for a person: start work, approve or deny the cards that need you, and see the day's receipts.

  • Each card says what will run, why it asked and what approving does.
  • Every decision files a receipt, chained into the record.

What the team sees · Tower

Business objects by reference

A Jira issue, a pull request, a hangar job: each is a chip, and each chip opens a page with what touched it and what is in play.

  • Tower keeps a reference, never a copy; title and state are read from the source.
  • The receipt carries the reference, hashed into the chain.

Built on · Airframe

One design system, every screen

Airframe holds the parts a person needs to work with an agent: approval card, question, composer, diff, object chips. Every screen in this deck is built from it.

  • Tokens and af- classes only; a test in each repo fails on a color literal.
